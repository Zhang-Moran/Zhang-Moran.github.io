# About Me
**Moran Zhang** is a doctoral student jointly trained by the National Academy of Artificial Intelligence and Zhejiang University. His research focuses on applications of artificial intelligence in biological systems. He has completed **eight academic publications**, including **seven SCI-indexed papers, five as first author**. He has received **seven national-level honors or awards**, including the National Scholarship and the Grand Prize in the National College Students' Life Science Competition. Research equipment for which he was principally responsible has been adopted by Peking University, Zhujiang Hospital of Southern Medical University, Shenzhen Kexing Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd., and other institutions. His publications have been selected for the recommended-reading sections of core textbooks in the Ministry of Education's “101 Plan” and Higher Education Press's 14th Five-Year Plan textbook series. He has led three projects, including projects supported by the Fundamental Research Funds for the Central Universities, and currently contributes to several projects, including the National Key Research and Development Program. His work focuses on methods and systems for intelligent sensing, modeling, analysis, and decision-making based on cross-scale, multimodal, and multi-scenario biological information.
